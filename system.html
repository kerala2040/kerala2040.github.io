Resource datasets retained as reference material

The source register includes ERA5 reanalysis, long-term modelled solar PVOUT and NIWE wind-resource records. These are meteorological or modelled resource datasets. They are not recorded installed generation, metered hourly output, or statutory renewable project capacity. See their original sources and data definitions.
