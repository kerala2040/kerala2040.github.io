Implications for electricity infrastructure

Elevation and monsoon rainfall support major hydropower reservoirs. Dense settlement compresses load and infrastructure into a relatively small land area. Forests, wetlands, steep terrain and hazard exposure constrain where new infrastructure can be built.

Interstate electricity imports

Kerala imports substantial electricity through the wider Indian grid. Interconnection is not itself a failure; the reliability question is whether enough supply and transfer capability are available at the difficult hour.

Annual consumption, category sales, periphery input and SLDC-reported imports use different accounting boundaries. The study therefore avoids manufacturing one universal “import dependence” percentage.

What is included

Hydropower, thermal, solar and wind generating capacity physically installed within Kerala. Interstate power contracts and imported electricity are excluded. Rooftop and distributed installations are not all individually enumerated in the asset register.

Installed MW cannot establish generation output or adequacy without dispatch, outages, hydro availability and demand chronology.
