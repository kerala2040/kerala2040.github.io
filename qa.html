Validation taxonomy

“Validation” is not one thing.

A conservation identity, a unit test and an out-of-sample physical comparison answer different questions.

ClassWhat it showsExample in Kerala2040

Material QA findings

The audit changed several claims.

Corrected

Shornur transformer

The 100 MVA input is a genuine 2023 KSEBL PSS entry, but current 2026 CEA planning reports 200+100 MVA at Shoranur. The hotspot geography survives and is independently corroborated; the old overload ratio is not current whole-station loading.

Corrected

Idukki v1.3 closure

The net-water forcing is reconstructed from storage change and generation. Historical storage replay therefore closes algebraically. This verifies implementation—not hydrology.

Narrowed

ERA5 load validation

The Jan–Mar test is a valid held-out intraday-shape evaluation conditional on known daily energy. It is not a day-ahead hourly demand forecast.

Narrowed

Network “robustness”

21 lines are robust only to the eight tested residual-import injection allocations—not to every load, dispatch, impedance, rating or contingency uncertainty.

Relabelled

IEX ₹5.24/kWh

It is the FY2023–24 average MCP (~₹5.2376/kWh), not the volume-weighted MCP (~₹5.1745/kWh).

Separated

5,904 vs 5,797 MW

5,904 MW is the generation-RA planning/calibration reference used by the proxy; 5,797 MW is the recorded FY2024–25 peak in the newer transmission-RA study. Different source contexts are kept separate.

Strongest conference claims

Claims that survived red-team review.

Strong

Historical growth

Official broad consumption grew from 22.54 to 29.31 TWh between FY2020–21 and FY2024–25.

Strong

Chronology matters

Screening produces renewable spill and shortage in the same year, so annual MWh alone cannot establish adequacy.

Strong sensitivity

Hydro timing matters

The same hydro energy has very different adequacy value depending on temporal freedom and transfer conditions.

Strong source

4,455 MW ATC

Current CEA Kerala transmission planning provides a dated 4,455 MW ATC reference.

Independent check

Shoranur geography

Current CEA planning independently corroborates the station/corridor area as constrained.

Methodological

Fail-closed gates

No invalid catchment, incomplete statutory GIS stack or unsupported future ATC is promoted merely to finish the model.

Red claims

Things this project must not say.

Not an event post-mortem

It does not prove the exact cause of a specific 2026 load-shedding episode.

Not “grid stability”

No transient, frequency, small-signal or voltage-stability study was performed.

Not an hourly forecast

The FY2024–25 demand chronology is reconstructed conditional on known daily energy.

Not validated hydrology

v1.3's historical storage closure is algebraic; Phase 5 catchment remains gated.

Not buildable GW

Ecological/statutory siting layers are not complete enough for a legal buildable-capacity claim.

Not an optimal 2040 portfolio

Economics are partial and key reliability/network/spatial gates remain open.

Reproducibility

Most model runs are reproducible; one important fit is only integrity-verifiable.

Reproducible layers

Historical transformations, PyPSA screens, hydro timing experiments, cross-solver comparisons, public network screens and synthetic flexibility pilots retain code/config/evidence sufficient to reproduce their released calculations subject to source access.

ERA5-sensitive v2 gap

The released 8,760 values, hashes, metadata and downstream use are reproducible. The original v2 coefficient-fitting script and raw ERA5 archive were not committed, so the fit cannot currently be regenerated from a fresh public clone.

How to challenge the work

The best answer is sometimes “I did not have evidence for that.”

Why not use a sophisticated AC or probabilistic model?
Because the missing input evidence—not solver capability—is the dominant limitation. A sophisticated model populated with invented bus loads, impedances, forced-outage rates or hydrology would create false precision.
Why should I trust a screening model?
Only for the question it was designed to answer. Sensitivities isolate mechanisms and can prioritize what deserves deeper measurement/modeling. They are not substitutes for operator-grade validation.
What is the project's biggest limitation?
Lack of synchronized operator-grade interval and spatial data. That forces the hourly demand and bus allocation to be reconstructed. The study responds by testing sensitivity and narrowing claims.
What would falsify the central synthesis?
Measured event-level data showing restrictions while dependable generation, external transfer and internal network headroom were all ample would mean the mechanism set is incomplete. Calibrated network evidence contradicting the hotspot geography would also force revision.
