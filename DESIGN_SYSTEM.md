# Kerala2040 visual system — Kasavu Research Edition
Updated 27 October 2026. Design reconstructed from the supplied homepage, theme and icon study.
Scientific evidence remains fixed at **23 October 2026**; redesign is not a new observational finding.

## Design vocabulary
- Forest green `#0F3A2C`: mastheads, scientific tables and footer.
- Kasavu gold `#C9A44C`: woven border, active navigation, scientific iconography and restrained emphasis.
- Cream `#F3ECDC` + paper `#FBF7EC`: reading backgrounds and cards.
- Fraunces for editorial research headlines, Inter for reading text, IBM Plex Mono for provenance/labels, Noto Serif Malayalam for native-language accents.
- Custom single-line 24×24 cultural and energy symbols in `assets/kerala-icons.svg`: palms, Western Ghats, houseboat, nilavilakku, kathakali, kasavu, pookalam, jackfruit, rain, hydro, solar, transmission, etc. These symbols are decorative, not evidence markers.
- `assets/kerala-horizon.svg`: Western Ghats / palms / transmission line drawing. Do not replace with generic generated energy artwork.

## Architecture
The public site has nine research pages: Overview, Research, System, Methods, Findings, Validation, Sources, Publication and Data access. Their scholarly content and source URLs were retained while the header, hero, call-to-action system, cards, tables, typography and footer were rebuilt. All pages share `assets/companion.css`.

## Scientific boundaries
The homepage's 29,311.76 MU (official FY2024–25 consumer-side electricity use), 4,412.14 MW (installed nameplate capacity) and 354/365 SLDC observed source days remain distinct categories. 353 same-day weather-matched targets and the seven-month 2024–25 ML exercise are *retrospective and exposed*, not operational or prospectively confirmed accuracy. Keep the 2026-10-23 release immutable in source history. The 2026–27 manuscript charter is a plan, not accepted research.

## Accessibility
Use semantic regions, descriptive labels, link focus rings, text-based cards, reduced-motion handling and responsive horizontal nav. The existing URL routes and anchor permalinks remain stable. Do not use icon-only navigation without labels.
