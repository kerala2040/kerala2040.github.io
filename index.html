Overview / independent research record

Kerala electricity: historical records and reproducible analyses

A frozen public research record covering source-reported electricity consumption, generation capacity, daily system statistics, and a retrospective daily demand–weather experiment. Results are organised by source, temporal resolution and method.

Research questions

Historical change

How did published annual electricity consumption and installed generation change over the documented period?

Daily operational records

How complete and internally consistent are the archived SLDC records for generation, consumption and interchange?

Weather and daily demand

Does same-day ERA5 reanalysis improve retrospective prediction of observed daily demand over a calendar-only baseline?

Hourly adequacy

What measured interval data are required to estimate intraday shortage mechanisms and operational hydro flexibility?

Scope of the frozen edition

The observational record does not contain a continuous independently measured Kerala hourly demand-and-dispatch series. Existing chronological adequacy, hydro-redispatch, transfer, network and 2040 scenario models are retained in the code repository as exploratory analyses. They are not part of this edition's reported system findings or investment conclusions.
