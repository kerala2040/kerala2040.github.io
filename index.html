06 · ABOUT

An independent public research platform.

What Kerala 2040 is

A transparent energy-system research project developed by Abhijith Sivaprasadan. The project began as an independent CET 2026 research build and is intended to grow into an open Kerala energy-data, modelling and investment-intelligence platform.

It is not an official Government of Kerala, KSEBL, regulatory or industry publication. Institutional participation or data sharing should be identified explicitly if added later.

Research principle

Energy sovereignty is not autarky. The objective is to reduce structural vulnerability while preserving economically useful interconnection and trade.
