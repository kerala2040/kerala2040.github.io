Methods / independent research record

Data preparation and retrospective modelling

The methodology distinguishes source-reported measurements from derived quantities and mathematical models. The current public analyses use annual statistics, audited daily records and observed daily targets for weather-model comparison.

Source collection and accounting

  1. Capture dated primary SLDC system-statistics pages and record per-response source SHA-256.
  2. Extract generation, consumption and interchange fields within the same report date and stated source definition.
  3. Retain unavailable days as missing in the observed dataset. Do not fill them when reporting observed totals.
  4. Apply the same-day energy-balance identity only to fields reported on a compatible operational boundary.
  5. Preserve the annual KSEBL-attributed consumption series independently of SLDC operational records.

Observed-daily demand and ERA5

The weather analysis uses 353 observed SLDC daily-consumption values matched to complete India-local ERA5 reanalysis days. The hourly reconstructed demand proxy is not used as a target or feature. Seven non-overlapping holdout months (September 2024–March 2025) are evaluated using expanding training windows. Calendar-only and calendar-plus-weather models are tuned independently with Optuna within each model family and fold.

Arithmetic verification and software reproducibility

The standalone daily-energy audit uses Python’s standard library to check observed source-day completeness, energy conservation in the released reconstructed hourly series, and correct classification of unavailable source days. These are data and implementation checks rather than evidence that the reconstructed hourly shape occurred.

Separation from exploratory models

Source-admitted daily evidence and retrospective ML results are the public study’s substantive findings. Previously developed hourly hydro, interconnection, capacity-expansion, network and siting models are preserved as exploratory methods in the codebase. They are excluded from the present findings because their physical inputs have not been independently established. Model inventory.
