Study design

  1. Identify primary sources, publication dates and the accounting boundary of every variable.
  2. Preserve original daily observations, including unavailable dates and source identifiers.
  3. Check energy identities within each compatible record population; do not combine annual consumer-side totals with SLDC operational totals.
  4. Evaluate the daily-demand weather model on chronological test months without using reconstructed hourly load as a training target.
  5. Keep scenario studies separate from the observational findings until suitable interval data permit external validation.
