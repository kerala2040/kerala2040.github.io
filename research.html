Two-minute overview

The project changed question before it changed answer.

Kerala2040 began as a broad transition-planning exercise. The evidence showed that a stronger scientific question came first: understand why the present system becomes stressed before deciding which technologies should be built.

Working synthesis: Kerala's susceptibility to power-system stress is better explained as an interaction among demand timing, weather, hydropower timing and availability, interstate transfer and internal deliverability than as one simple annual-energy deficit.

Research questions

Six questions, in a deliberate order.

The first five diagnose the present system. The sixth asks what should be tested next.

RQ1

How has the electricity balance changed?

Demand growth, internal generation and electricity arriving from outside Kerala are kept in their own official accounting boundaries.

RQ2

Annual energy problem or difficult-hour problem?

Chronology, peak timing and weather-sensitive demand are reconstructed because annual MWh alone cannot show when supply and demand fail to coincide.

RQ3

How valuable is hydro flexibility?

Counterfactual experiments hold hydro energy fixed and change only when it can be used.

RQ4

How vulnerable is Kerala to external supply?

Current transfer capability and price boundaries are separated from synthetic transfer-stress cases.

RQ5

Can statewide balance hide internal constraints?

A public multibus screen tests whether line/corridor stress survives radically different interstate-injection allocations.

RQ6

Which responses deserve deeper study?

Hydro operation, demand flexibility, storage, targeted reinforcement and new supply are candidate mechanisms, not a finished portfolio.

Study logic

Evidence ladder, not one giant model.

Each layer answers only the question its data can support. Higher sophistication is admitted only when the underlying evidence exists.

01

Audit the source boundary

Preserve dates, definitions, missingness and contradictory records rather than forcing all official numbers to agree.

02

Reconstruct only what is missing

Daily energy is observed; intraday shape is reconstructed. Missing observed days stay explicitly model-only.

03

Test mechanisms separately

Transfer, hydro timing, candidate capacity and network injection are varied one mechanism at a time before synthesis.

04

Cross-check implementation

PyPSA, a custom SciPy formulation and OSeMOSYS are compared where the mathematical contracts overlap.

05

Seek independent physical corroboration

For example, current CEA transmission planning is used to challenge—not merely confirm—the public network screen.

06

Fail closed

No validated catchment, buildable-MW ceiling, N-1 result or optimal portfolio is manufactured when the required evidence is absent.
