Criteria for reopening analysis

The main unresolved observational requirement is a compatible measured Kerala interval-demand and dispatch dataset, with source-defined electricity boundaries, timestamp conventions and reuse rights. Corrections to primary-source arithmetic and independent external-year validations may also warrant revision. New simulation detail alone is insufficient.

From evidence to publication

Paper A / retrospective weather study

Qualified daily Kerala SLDC operational consumption and independently audited five-point ERA5 reanalysis. Test incremental historical weather information against fixed calendar-only Ridge baselines in expanding-origin fiscal-year backtests. Positive, null and negative years all count.

Paper A0 / source-accounting fallback

A source-vintage, accounting-boundary and missingness methods study, with an actual January 2027 prospectus and novelty check. If journal-level novelty is absent, publish an accurately labelled technical report, not a forced paper.

Retrospective chronological test folds are not automatically independent prospective confirmations. FY2024–25 is already analysis-exposed, and other historical years require an exposure audit. ERA5 is same-day reanalysis, not a day-ahead forecast.

Scientific integrity / publication route

What remains conditional

Source custody and authorisation

Private source days and restricted meteorological originals are not bulk-publication material without a separate rights decision. An SLDC/KSEBL source request is drafted but not automatically sent.

Peer review is not yet achieved

Any manuscript, preprint, repository DOI or technical report must be labelled by its actual review status. Researcher affiliation must be current and genuine. Baseline personal article-processing-charge commitment: €0 unless explicitly changed.

Measured hourly demand/dispatch, real-world LOLE, specific shortage causes, independently demonstrated hydro flexibility and an optimal least-cost Kerala 2040 portfolio remain outside the admitted scientific claims.
