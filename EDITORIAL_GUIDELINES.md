# Website editorial guidelines

Kerala2040 is an independent research project. The public site should read like a researcher's field and modelling report, with a clear identity and enough detail to be useful. Neutrality does not mean writing generic section labels.

- Use specific, engaging research headings that name a real question, dataset, unit, method or result. Avoid slogans and motivational catchphrases, but also avoid generic placeholders such as “Research status” or “Results and sensitivity analyses.”
- Describe what a model did before evaluating significance. Distinguish published observations, reconstructed data, assumptions, tests and inferences.
- Do not award the project its own credentials. Internal QA and solver agreement are not peer review, physical validation or evidence that a paper will be accepted.
- Give numerical results with units, source periods, assumptions and model scope. Do not present screening overloads as measured network conditions.
- State important limitations where they affect interpretation and link to Validation for the full account, rather than repeating disclaimers throughout every page.
- Describe interventions as hypotheses for further study, not investment recommendations from an uncalibrated model.
- Retain citations, links, navigation anchors and evidence provenance when revising copy. Do not alter numerical evidence during an editorial pass.
- Material from the withdrawn CET 2026 poster should stay in clearly archived notes, not in the current public research narrative.
- Preserve the visual identity: Playfair Display for display and section headings, DM Sans for body text, and DM Mono for scientific labels. The Malayalam brand mark remains unchanged. Do not remove a font or replace it with a fallback without explicit design review.
- Maintain existing CI checks and deployment requirements when modifying page structure.
