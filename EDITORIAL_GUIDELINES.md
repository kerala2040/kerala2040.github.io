# Editorial policy for the frozen research record

1. Write factually. Give the measured or published variable, units, date, institution and system boundary before reporting any numerical value.
2. Separate official source-reported, derived arithmetic, retrospective statistical model, and exploratory simulation. Only the first three are within the present public findings, with the model classification visible.
3. Do not present scenario unserved energy, hydro dispatch freedom, assumed ATC reductions, hypothetical ramp constraints, investment technologies, speculative capacity or land-suitability filters as Kerala operating facts.
4. Distinguish the State Planning Board's consumer-side annual electricity consumption from SLDC's operational daily consumption. The two are not interchangeable.
5. Avoid rhetorical questions, slogans, claims of original discovery, promotional buttons and self-evaluation. Publish methods and observed results, not account of past editorial corrections.
6. Describe missing measurements once in the relevant scope/methods; do not scatter defensive disclaimers after every statement.
7. Revisions of frozen results require a source/definition correction, newly admitted primary observation or independent validation, recorded with a new snapshot and review notes.
8. Do not publish old generated scenario dashboards, conference coaching pages or unused dashboard JavaScript as part of this edition.
9. Maintain the site’s link/data/claims checks in CI and the immutable research-data pin. A successful deployment does not imply independent physical validation.
